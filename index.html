Why the forum exists

Nursing is evolving. Your professional development should evolve with it.

Healthcare opportunities increasingly reward nurses who combine strong clinical foundations with specialized skills, professional visibility, adaptability and networks. The Forum brings those growth areas together in one professional community.

Members can access information about training, mentorship, specialized nursing areas, home healthcare, professional networking, career development and other opportunities relevant to modern nursing practice.
